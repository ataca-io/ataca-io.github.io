Services

Three services. One provider.

Each one does a single job well. Use them alone or together.

connect

Omnichannel messaging

One API for transactional notifications and customer conversations. Set a cascade policy and connect tries the next channel when the first one fails.

  • SMS, WhatsApp, Viber and email
  • Cascade delivery policies
  • Two-way communication
  • Delivery tracking
connect.ataca.io

rail

Transactional email

Send notifications, alerts and receipts over SMTP or HTTP. Or point a static site's contact form at rail and get submissions as email, with no backend.

  • SMTP relay, HTTP API, HTML forms
  • mTLS auth, DKIM-signed delivery
  • Inbound email to webhooks
  • Spam protection, open tracking, live status
rail.ataca.io

depot

Short-term file storage

Upload a file over mTLS and get back an expiring link. Put the link in the email instead of the attachment: no base64 bloat, no copy left in every mailbox.

  • Streaming upload with live progress
  • Per-file TTL, seven days by default
  • Browser download, no login needed
  • Expired links answer 410 Gone
Ask about depot

Better together

rail sends the email, depot serves the file. The recipient gets a link that works for seven days and then answers 410 Gone.
